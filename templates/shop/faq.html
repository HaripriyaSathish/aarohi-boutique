Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the right size?

Use the guide on the product page. For exact garment measurements, contact the store before ordering.

Which payment methods are available?

Live checkout uses Razorpay. Enabled methods depend on the store’s gateway configuration. Local demo checkout does not collect money.

How do I track an order?

Open My Account, choose an order and view tracking events. Courier tracking appears after dispatch.

Can I cancel or exchange?

Cancellation is allowed before packing. Eligible delivered items support return or size-exchange requests.
